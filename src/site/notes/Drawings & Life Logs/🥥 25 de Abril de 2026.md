:::lang en

This one was a **tough nut to crack**. Most of it I had to **redo** and **redraw**, besides being the kind of drawing where, if you **mess** with the position or proportion of one element, it altered or even revealed a **flaw** in another.

Another detail noticed by the teacher is the shape of the **skull**, in the case of this specific reference: instead of a **circular** shape at the back of the character's head, like the previous ones, this one has an **ellipse** shape (or, in the teacher's words, a coconut), which is quite evident in her hair.

![22Desenho.jpg](/img/user/img/Drawings/22Desenho.jpg)

I noticed the **giant** size of her eye.
